# UI pass 2: title, game split, lobby picker, one-frame Career (2026-10-16)

The second menu pass after the 2026-10-16 refinement (#2121-#2124). It started from a prod audit at 1920x1080 and
1366x768, and the owner then steered it item by item.

## What shipped

- **Title.** The 264px letter disc became a compact identity card at the top right:
  - big portrait with its frame, and the account level on the ring;
  - big name, then one rank line (crest, division, small MMR);
  - the title pill and XP bar stay on Career.

  Play is the hero call to action. Report a Problem and the build stamp moved to the bottom left; the stamp used to
  overlap the help and book buttons. A "What's new" card was built and then removed at the owner's call.
- **Patch Notes** use the navy and bronze menu panel. They were the one cream and orange surface left.
- **Settings over the menus** closes with a muted "Done" (gold stays Play's alone). In a game it is still the gold
  "Resume".
- **Leaderboard** says MMR everywhere.
- **Game split** (`GameSplit.tsx`) is shared by Recent Games and the Career's Match History and Practice tabs: a
  compact list (Up and Down to move) beside the selected game in full.
  - The detail is `GameCard` in a `gc-detail` layout: actions on the head row, the board sized from the card width
    (up to 170px), the runes as named pills, and the meta line.
  - Only the open game mounts a board, so the lazy-row machinery left both lists.
  - Career Stats collapsed into a strip across the top so the detail has room.
- **Lobby picker** (`matchDetails/LobbyPicker.tsx`). The owner picked the tile table over seat chips.
  - The big board shows your board. A tile in the 4 x 2 lobby swaps in that seat's board and runes, with a crossfade.
  - A "Showing" line says whose board it is, and "Back to yours" returns.
  - Places read 1st to 8th, or "Still in".
  - Picking a tile below the fold scrolls the board into view.
  - The end screen's Match details dialog keeps `MatchScoreboard`.
- **Ranked standings on Recent Games** are loaded on demand by `fetchRecentMatch`:
  - first the telemetry row's `replay->>seed` (one row);
  - then that player's `run_history` row with the same `entry->>seed` (one row, projecting `entry->match`), cached.

  `run_history` is already readable by every signed-in session (schema.sql 2026-08-04 "read run_history"), so no
  migration or RPC was needed.
- **One frame, no page scroll** on Career and Recent Games at 1920x1080 and 1366x768. Only the game list scrolls.
  The MMR chart tip shows that run's final board (`MiniBoard`, shared with the tiles) and seats itself inside the
  panel.
- **Practice**: the Sandbox and God Mode tiles no longer move. The screen is top-aligned and the options panel keeps
  the Sandbox height.
- **Mode screen**: `hall-soft.webp`, a third plate from `build-menu-backdrop.mjs` (a 3px blur, baked).
- **Audit fixes**:
  - one page header size;
  - the account panel in the menu system (no full-screen backdrop-filter);
  - Collection says how to get an item once, and its filters align left;
  - Heroes cards show Top 4, average place and 1sts;
  - the Gauntlet shows one coming-soon line;
  - the Live Games empty state links Recent Games;
  - a `warn` icon for error states.

## Perf notes

- Recent Games' detail board missed the Career-only FX pause and idled at 81 running animations. `.gc:not(:hover)`
  now covers every game card.
- The Reborn wisps and dome stay paused on menu boards even on hover. A hover trace showed about 46k style
  invalidations from their main-thread keyframes, and pausing them cut hover style from 741 ms to about 185 ms per
  3 s sweep.
- The game list's hover slide was removed. It cost layout on every pass over a 50-row scroller.
- The remaining over-budget hover frames on Recent Games come from the shared card hover-preview mount (script). They
  are not menu-specific.
