## New mechanics

- **Perfect Vision**: `spellSetStats` takes optional `atTier` / `tierAttack` / `tierHealth`. One helper, `setStatsValue`
  (recruit.ts), answers both the cast (the run's CURRENT Shop Tier) and `spellDisplayText`, which greens whichever value
  applies right now. R-SETSTATS-TIER-01.
- **Strange Revision**: new factory `spellTransformTierUp` (replaces `spellTransformSameTier`, which had no other user).
  The destination tier is `strangeRevisionTier(tier) = min(7, tier + 1)`, drawn from `poolOf(state).buyable` in the run
  tribes off the run cursor. `castSpellOnOffer` now keeps a Displaced body's stats instead of re-basing its bonus.
  R-TRANSFORM-01.

## Judgement calls (owner calls pending)

- **Taragosa now casts Growth for +2/+2 (was +3/+4).** Its `onAllyAttackCastGrowth` params were a stale copy of Growth
  from before #622 (Growth went +3/+4 to +1/+1 then and Taragosa never followed), so the "cast Growth" text and its hover
  preview disagreed with what it gave. Re-synced to the spell, with a tripwire test pinning the two together. If the
  owner wants Taragosa to stay at +3/+4, the text needs to stop saying "Growth" instead.
- **Strange Revision on a Tier 7** re-rolls into a different Tier 7, following the Skybound Ascendant ruling of
  2026-09-09, rather than refusing the target.
- **What Strange Revision keeps**: the body's stored Attack/Health (no stat-gain watcher fires), golden status, the
  enchantment ledger, per-instance counters and gained keywords. The old card's printed keywords swap for the new card's.
  A run-wide Undead aura that only applied to the old identity stops showing on a non-Undead form (stored stats are
  kept, not the displayed fold).

## Other
