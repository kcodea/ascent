## Reworks and moves

- **Solburn**: `onSell` instead of `onPlay`, same `battlecryCollapseStarform` body ("Sell: Collapse your Starform.").
  The sale removes Solburn from the board before `fireOnSell`, so it is never one of its own hits (before, it was
  eligible). Stats kept at 4/5. Policy `factory:battlecryCollapseStarform:onSell` (economy); the onPlay row went.
- **Shredder**: "End of Turn: give your minions +2/+3 for every Equipment unused this turn." The shared factory
  takes `scope: 'all'`; per-Equipment waves, captures and gain-Attack notifications are unchanged. Live text
  (`shredderText`) prints the current total on both chains. Gilded +4/+6.
- **Blaster** T4 to T3. **Calibration Master** T5 9/6 to T4 6/4.

## New cards (Set 3 Neutrals, no art: the default missing-art frame)

- **Indy** (`n3_indy`, T4 5/5): `shopRefreshed` → `onShopRefreshAddToShop { every: 5, cardId: 'n3_goldenmonkey' }`.
  Per-copy `eotTick` meter, cumulative from arrival, repeats every 5. Owner follow-up the same day: "this should only
  count hitting the refresh button", so only the reducer's `roll` action passes `playerRoll` to `applyShopRefreshed`
  and the factory ignores every other refresh (turn-start row, runes, spells, hero powers). Free rerolls still count:
  they are the player pressing Refresh. The board badge counts the refreshes down (the shared `stepProgress` refresh branch). Gilded adds 2.
  `addCardToShop` never grows the row: a free slot first, else it swaps the right-most minion (never the
  Starform, a spell or another Monkey); the displaced offer goes back to the pool.
- **Golden Monkey** (`n3_goldenmonkey`, token, T1 6/6 Taunt): `onSellDiscover { tierOffset: 1 }`: a fixed-tier
  Discover at Shop tier + 1, capped at 7 (honoured with no Tier 7 access, like Salvatore / the Summit rune).
  Gilded offers gilded minions. Live text `sellDiscoverTierText` names the tier.
- **Amplification Agnus** (`n3_agnus`, T7 10/10): a `passive` marker `equipmentAmplifyAll`, read by
  `equipmentAmplifiedByAura` in the activation and in `equipmentWillAmplify` (the blue slot). Spends nothing: a
  banked stack or a pending Calibration is kept. No rune is credited for an Agnus Amplification.

Oracle: R-INDY-01, R-AGNUS-01, R-SOLBURN-01 (`registry/approved/triggers.ts`). Tests: `set3Batch1013.test.ts`, plus
the Shredder / Calibration pins in `set3NeutralEquipment.test.ts`.
