# The Crit beam (2026-11-13)

Owner: "now im making a crit beam. fx is crit-beam".

- **Critical Strike joins the generic keyword launcher** (`fx/keywordBeam.ts`, `KEYWORD_BEAMS.CR` -> `crit-beam`), so
  a spell (pointer or hero portrait), a hero power and a rune send it from the same origins as the other keywords.
- **The Shop.** Mid-turn it is a board read in `Recruit.tsx` (a minion that did not have `CR` and now does); at End
  of Turn it is the `keywordChanged` cue with a rune or hero origin (Vanguard, Centerline).
- **Combat (owner: "i thought i was very clear that these effects should come from runes").** Vanguard and Centerline
  pushed `CR` onto the minion in `simulate.ts` without logging it, so the replay had nothing to launch from. They now
  log a `keyword` event, the way a rune-given Flurry already did. A new event in the combat log: Kevin's side of the
  seam, changed on the owner's ask. `runeKeywordBeams.ts` pairs it with the rune's fire by step.
- **Banked spells.** A keyword a next-combat spell gives as the fight opens (Executioner's Edge, Last Stand) leaves
  where the spell's cast card shows (`bankedKeywordGrantsIn`).
- **No hold.** Critical Strike has no look on the card to hold back.
- **Sounds.** ElevenLabs sound generation, twelve takes over four prompts. Defaults are the first blade shing (launch)
  and the first impact (landing), picked unheard; both at 0.8 with a 7% pitch jitter. The owner auditions the rest.
- No minion gives another minion Critical Strike, so there is no "On Crit given" binding kind.
- Also fixed: the launcher's hold fell through to the Ward hold for any keyword it did not name.
