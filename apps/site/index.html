Play on your time

Take the time you need

You fight other players' boards, and nobody else has to be online when you are. The clock gives you room to think.

  1. The clock stops for big choices

    While a Discover, the Runeforge or Ancient select is open, your timer waits until you pick.

  2. The Gold Fuse

    Your turn timer does not start until you have spent a set amount of Gold.

  3. Out of time? Keep arranging.

    When your time is up, you can still rearrange your board before the fight.

  4. Come back to your game

    Save and quit, then continue where you left off.

The Runeforge

Runes that last the whole run

Each time the forge opens it offers four runes and you take one. Its power stays with you for the rest of the run.

Some runes hand you a card. Pick one to see what it gives.

Card Reward Chimerus

Tier 6 Dragon · 4 Attack 8 Health

Rally: give this minion's Health to 2 friendly Dragons.

Card Reward Voidmother

Tier 6 Beast · 6 Attack 1 Health

Echo: summon a Void Panther.

Card Reward Bucky

Tier 6 Dwarf · 6 Attack 10 Health

Start of Combat: give your Dwarves +5/+5 for every Dwarven Ale you cast last turn.

Card Reward Arcane Behemoth

Tier 6 Demon · 6 Attack 10 Health

When you Sell a Demon, this gains its stats.

Card Reward Yazzus

Tier 7 · 4 Attack 8 Health

Tier 4 Kobold · 3 Attack 7 Health

The Ancients

Summon an Ancient, change your hero

Earn 16 points to summon one: 1 for every shop roll and 2 for every combat round you finish. Then Discover three of your hero's six Ancients. The one you choose changes your hero power for the rest of the game.

Ancient of Bonds

Links your minions together, so a buff or a trigger on one spreads to the others.

In-game Example

IndyMasterwork also gives +20/+20 for every Gilded minion this game. It can target Gilded minions.

Ancient of Death

Turns dying into an advantage. Minions come back, and you are rewarded when they fall.

In-game Example

IndyMasterwork targets gain Rebirth and Taunt.

Ancient of Fortune

All about Gold. Earn more of it, spend less of it, and get things for free.

In-game Example

IndySelling a Gilded minion gets you a plain copy of it.

Ancient of Genesis

Makes more of what you have. Extra copies of your minions, and more to Discover.

In-game Example

IndyMasterwork gets 2 plain copies of a chosen minion instead.

Ancient of Time

Changes when your power works. Effects at the start of a turn or a combat, and things that happen twice.

In-game Example

IndyStart of Combat: your right-most minion becomes Gilded for that combat.

Ancient of War

Raw fighting power. Bigger stats, extra attacks and harder hits in combat.

In-game Example

IndyGilded minions gain +8/+8 when a friendly minion dies, permanently.
