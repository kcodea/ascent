# 2026-09-24: Effects are no longer clipped by a fixed particle box

Owner (verbatim, with a screenshot of a purple haze around a lone enemy minion, sliced by a hard vertical edge):
*"noticing effects gettin cut off in certain cases like this if a board isn't full or something. these effects
shouldnt be cut off by such restrictions and should play their full area/animation unless that somehow breaks
something."*

## Root cause

`packages/ui/src/fx/particleLayerPool.ts` gave every particle layer (burst, emitter, smoke) a fixed
`boundsArea` of `Rectangle(-2000, -2000, 4000, 4000)`. A `ParticleContainer` computes no bounds of its own, so
that box IS its bounds, and Pixi's `FilterSystem` sizes a filter's render texture from
`container.getFastGlobalBounds()` (then clips it to the viewport). So every blurred or Filter Lab particle layer
could only ever draw inside x, y in [-2000, 2000] (in the layer's local space). Anything past it was cut dead
straight.

On a viewport up to 2000 CSS px wide that box is bigger than the screen, so nothing showed. On a wider screen
(2560x1440 and up, where the owner plays; the card size in the screenshot matches a ~2500 px viewport) the
right side of the screen sat outside it. A lone minion is centred, so its haze spills far to the right and
hits the line. That is why it looked like a board-count thing. The board count only moves where the minion sits.

Candidates checked and ruled out: the FX canvases (`resizeTo: window`, `.pixifx` / `.pixifx-below` are
`position: fixed; inset: 0`), DOM ancestors of the unit (no `overflow`, `contain`, `clip-path` or transform
on the `.unit` / `.row` / `.zone` / `.app` chain), Pixi masks (only the `custom` primitive's opt-in mask role),
render textures (none on the FX path), and the mesh primitives (their filter area is their own geometry, which
contains what they draw).

## Fix

The box is now `PARTICLE_BOUNDS_HALF_EXTENT = 1e6` on each side, so the only bound left is the one Pixi
applies anyway: the viewport (`clipToViewport`, on for every filter we build, core and pixi-filters). The
viewport, not the board or a guess about mote travel, is the bound.

## Verification

Live on a dev server (port 5197), combat with a lone enemy minion, a wide blurred burst fired on it, filter
bounds read by wrapping `renderer.filter._calculateFilterBounds`:

| Viewport | Filter area before | Filter area after |
|---|---|---|
| 1280x800 | -10..1290 x -10..810 | -10..1290 x -10..810 (same) |
| 2560x1440 | -16..**2016** x -16..1456 | -16..**2576** x -16..1456 |

Before: every mote right of x = 2000 was missing. After: they render to the screen edge.

## Performance

Up to a 2000 px viewport the filter texture is byte-for-byte the same size, so there is zero change. On a wider
viewport a blurred particle layer now renders the whole screen, which is the point of the fix: the texture grows
by the area that used to be cut (2000x1440 to 2560x1440, +28% for that pass). Measured at 2560x1440 with
`growth-effect` live (dev build, 40 synchronous renders each forced to finish with a 1 px `readPixels`, so these
are upper bounds and include the other FX live in the scene): median 7.1 ms before, 8.4 ms after; worst frame
11.4 ms before, 11.2 ms after (warm runs). No new looping paint, no per-frame layout reads.

## Enforcement

`packages/ui/src/fx/particleLayerPool.test.ts`: the layer's bounds must contain the whole viewport (1280x720,
2560x1440, 3840x2160) for heads at the centre, near a corner and at the origin, under the scale / drift / spin
envelope `transformEnvelope.ts` applies. 16 of those cases fail against the old +/-2000 box. Oracle rule
R-PRESENT-12 in `packages/rules/src/registry/approved/foundation.ts`.
