**Judgement calls.**
- *"The left-most minion in your hand"* skips spells and Rubies (Chosen Vessel's reading). In combat it is the fight's
  hand snapshot (`handMinionsFor`), so a card that reached the hand mid-fight is not a target until settle.
- *The Kindred Hand's live text* is the badge: `next +N/+N`, what the next Spirit you play pays (the board's Spirits plus
  that one), per copy. The payout counts the played Spirit, since it is on the board when the rune reads.
- *The Encore* relays exactly what the sold Reveler paid the board (a Flame Reveler: Attack only), per copy. A Reveler
  whose pay was 0/0 does not spend the charge.
- *The Anvil* reads the Attack actually gained: the shop's action-boundary diff, the Striker / Kringle per-tick waves
  and the End-of-Turn projection now pass `gained` to `fireOnGainAttack`. Board Dwarves only (the Kneel ruling).
- *The Satchel in combat* buffs the living Dwarves for the fight; when the granted card reaches the run hand at settle,
  its arrival pays the board permanently. That is the same double shape Gangplank (`onGainCardBuffTribe`) already
  has; flagged in case the owner wants combat arrivals to skip the Shop half.
- *The Whetstone* gives Attack only and never the played Dwarf.
