## Unity: the prototype

"While you control all **5** minion types, your minions count as **every type**."

- **The condition reads natural types only** (`isTribeNatural` in the Shop; printed tribes and All-types defs in combat,
  the Stoked Menagerie's reading). Unity's own grant never counts, so it cannot hold itself up: sell or lose the last
  Celestial and every minion stops being a Celestial at once.
- **"Your minions" is the board.** Hand cards are never marked.
- **Shop.** `syncUnity` stamps or clears `unityTribes` on every board minion. It runs at every reducer action boundary
  and at the Shop chokepoints that change the board mid-action: `playCard` (before the played body's triggers read
  tribes), `fireOnMinionSold`, `fireOnFriendDeath` and `applyEndOfTurn`. `isTribe` reads the mark; the badge prints
  `n/5 types`; the card shows the All pill while it holds. The mark is Shop-only (snapshot registry excuse): a served
  board re-derives Unity in combat from `questMods.runeUnity`.
- **Combat.** `syncUnityCombat` runs on the starting boards (before Start of Combat), after every death
  (`noteCardDeath`, which every death path calls) and on every summon (`summonEntryEffects`). While the side holds the
  full house its living minions carry `universalTribe`; when it breaks, Unity's marks are withdrawn (a printed All-types
  body keeps its own).
- **Combat tribe checks now honour the per-instance mark.** Many combat checks read only the card def's
  `universalTribe`. They now read `m.universalTribe || def.universalTribe` (45 sites in `simulate.ts` and
  `factories.ts`, a purely additive change: it only newly answers true where the body is marked). This also makes an
  Anomaly Reactor "All" body count as every tribe in those checks, which is what its mark always claimed. Owner flag:
  that is a (set 1) behaviour change riding this PR.
- **The Undead Aura.** A Unity body counts as Undead, so it takes the whole Aura (Lantern and buy Attack, Health) as a
  fold (`unityAuraFold` in `foldedAuraOf` and the board view), never baked: the buy-channel bakes now read
  `isTribeNatural`, so nothing is counted twice. Combat hands it over once at the grant (a Grim Toast Dwarf and a
  natural Undead already have it) and does not take it back if the house breaks mid-fight.

**Judgement calls.** Unity covers the board, not the hand; the Aura is not clawed back mid-fight; the Heavy Hand's
second copy adds one more share (x3), the "engines double their output" duplicate family; Unity joins the duplicate
sweetener (a second copy changes nothing). Heavy Hand only acts in combat, where all damage is dealt; its tally carries
back as every Pummel tally does.
