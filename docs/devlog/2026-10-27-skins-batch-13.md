## Names

The names are the filenames, except these:
- **Frontier Prospector** (was Gold Rush Prospector): the catalog bans ranked medal words like "Gold".
- **Cathedral Lastlight** (was Stained Glass Lastlight) and **Poly Flutterbutter** (was Low Poly Flutterbutter): names
  are capped at 20 characters.
- **Brick Tapkeeper** (was Lego Tapkeeper): "Lego" is a brand name.
- **Glass Guardian** and **Ghillie Sharpshooter** have their spelling tidied.

The owner can rename any of them; the ids stay.
