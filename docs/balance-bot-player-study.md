## Findings (hand-written; the generator preserves this block)

Read the tables below for the numbers; these are the things they say that a designer (or the next pilot session) should act on.

1. **The field's growth is exponential from wave 7 and it is goldens + engines, not bodies.** Median total stats
   19 → 31 → 53 → 88 → 139 → 248 → 432 → 821 → 1,109 over waves 4 → 12 (a ×1.6–1.9 step every wave from wave 7),
   while the board is already 6+ bodies at wave 7 and 7 at wave 10 — after wave 7 NOTHING comes from adding bodies.
   Goldens per board go 0.2 → 0.5 → 0.7 → 1.1 → 1.6 over waves 6 → 13 (p80 = 2–3 late); shop tier is 4.6 at wave 8
   and 5.3 at wave 10. The strategist's boards (83 / 133 / 253 at waves 8 / 10 / 12) sit at the corpus' p20 or
   below from wave 7 on: it holds as many bodies as the players, of the same tiers, and gets a third of the stats.

2. **The cards that predict survival are ENGINES that need to be fed, not bodies.** Bob Blart (Demon consume: holders
   at waves 5–7 went on 9.8 more waves vs 5.5 for non-holders, +79%; 100% of the 15 holders survived), Storm Chaser
   (+34% / +77% at build / scale), Kennelmaster (+41%), Broad-Axe Brakka (+51% at scale), Echohorn (+57%), Soul Defiler,
   Paragon, Gemheart Carver. Every one of them scales with something the OWNER does each turn (consumes, spells cast,
   summons, Ales, Rallies). Holding the card is not the engine; feeding it is (the B7 imitation experiment proved
   exactly this — see docs/balance-bot.md).

3. **Early filler that stays on the board is the strongest death signal.** Embermouth Whelp on a wave 5–7 board:
   4% survivor rate (base 53%), the run lasted 2.7 more waves vs 6.2. Chipwick Prospector 16%, Buddy Buddy 17%,
   Ancient Wanderer 25%, Packstrider, Cheap Date, Void Panther all well below base. The pattern is a player who did
   not turn the board over by wave 6. Cinderchef is the exception: a wave-1 card the survivors KEEP (69% at open,
   61% at build, 42% golden by waves 5–7) because it is an engine.

4. **The end-game boards are a small, tribe-locked vocabulary.** Final boards of the 20 runs that reached wave 14+
   are dominated by Vaultkeeper (7 of 20, 0 of the early-outs), Sylus (7), Lastlight (6, 0 early), Echohorn (6),
   Tapkeeper (5, 0 early), Brakka, Thane, Edward, Transcendant; Drakko and Chorus Drake appear on BOTH ends (Drakko
   5 / 5, Chorus Drake 3 end / 7 early) — the Dragon Rally shell reaches wave 10 reliably and then stalls without
   Vaultkeeper. The strongest late PAIRS are Chorus Drake + Vaultkeeper (18 boards, 100% survivor), Fel Spikes +
   Sylus (15, 100%), Vaultkeeper + Drakko (14, 100%). Lines players actually took, by dominant tribe on the final
   board: Dragon 18 runs (33% reached the end), Demon 16 (25%), Dwarf 15 (27%), Beast 12 (33%), Kobold 9 (22%) —
   no tribe is a trap, and Beast + Dragon finish a wave later on average.

5. **The corpus is too thin for per-hero conclusions and too concentrated for pair conclusions.** 70 runs over ~40
   heroes (max 4 per hero), 8 authors — but TWO of them wrote 87% of the boards (53% and 34%), so "the players" is mostly two people’s habits — and 35 patch stamps. Per-card weights fit on it
   hold up out-of-sample only modestly (run-split AUC 0.61 / 0.63 / 0.60 at open / build / scale; the late band is
   noise), so treat every "X survives" row with fewer than ~15 boards as a lead. A second corpus fetch after more
   uploads (`balance:corpus`) is the cheapest way to sharpen all of the above.
