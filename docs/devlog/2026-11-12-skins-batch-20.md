## Names, for the owner to confirm

- **Priestess Sweeney** follows the other four Sweeney hero skins.
- **Hand Drawn Priestess** follows the Hand Drawn card plates (two words).
- **Minecraft Priestess** is the file name. "Minecraft" is another company's trademark in a player-facing name. Kept
  as is and flagged; renaming is a one-word change to `name` (the id carries no such word).

## Priestess is Duos only

Nothing filters a Duos-only hero's skins. They drop from any chest like every other hero skin, show in the
Collection's Heroes tab under "Priestess" and can be equipped there. The skin is only seen in a game where Priestess
can be picked: Versus Duos and Ranked Duos. Behaviour unchanged.
