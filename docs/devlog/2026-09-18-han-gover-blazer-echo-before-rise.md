## Han Gover — a new Set 3 Dwarf/Undead (`dw3_hangover`)

Tier 4, 4/7, `tribe: 'dwarf', tribe2: 'undead'`. *"When this deals 40 damage, get an Ale."* Gilded: 2 Ales.

**How it works.** A `passive` marker effect, `dealtDamageAleMeter { every: 40, count: 1 }`, in the Spear Warden
`cardDeathScaler` mould — not a new trigger. The combat damage site (`applyDamage` in `simulate.ts`, right after
the `dmg` event) calls `noteDamageDealt(dealer, amount)`: a body whose card carries the marker adds the FULL
amount of every landed hit (attacks, retaliation, incidental; never an Immune / popped-Ward / 0-damage hit) to
its per-instance `Minion.damageDealt`. Every multiple of 40 the tally crosses grants `count` (×2 gilded) random
Dwarven Ales through `ctx.grantToHand` — the standard combat→hand channel, so the replay shows the card flying
to hand on the hit, and settle lands it in the real hand through `playerHandGrants`. One enormous hit can cross
two thresholds and pays both. Overkill counts.

**Persistence.** The tally is seeded from the run card (`BoardCard.damageDealt` → `BoardMinion` → `Minion`, via
`minion.ts` and the reducer's board→combat mapping) and carried back on a new settle field,
`CombatResult.playerDamageMeters: { sourceUid, total }[]` (`CombatCarryBacks.damageMeters`; the enemy half is
mirrored). `settleCombat` writes `total` back onto the board card, so the meter survives combat → shop →
combat. Snapshots (`snapshot.ts` / `opponents.ts`) carry it so a served Han Gover pays out from its real total;
a triple keeps the highest tally of the merged copies (Tara's rule). The seat runner and the symmetric-carry
test map the new field; the snapshot-fidelity exemplars and the live-tracking audit classify it.

**Text.** Per the owner's 2026-09-11 ruling (*trackers, not fractions in the text*), the progress is the
step-counter badge (`stepProgress` → `damageDealt`, cyclic 1..40), on the board (`instView`) and in combat
(`Unit.tsx`; `useCombatReplay` sums the `dmg` events stamped with him as `source` onto the seeded value — the
same amounts the sim added). The printed sentence never scales, so it is registered `accurate-at-any-value` in
`renderedText.registry.ts`. The carry-back's first name, `playerDamageDealt`, collided with the replay-v2
`CombatFrame` field of that name (the lobby's damage-to-opponent) — hence `playerDamageMeters`.

**Not built:** a shop half. No minion deals damage in the shop, so the recruit factory is a no-op marker.

## Echo first, THEN the Rise attempts (reverses R-RISE-05 of 2026-09-09)

Owner report: a Deathfibrillated minion on a 7-body board *"rises BEFORE its Echo triggers"*. Under the
2026-09-09 ruling a rising body HELD its slot through its Echo, so the Echo's summon overflowed and the body
returned — which is exactly what the player saw. New law, for ALL Rise/Echo interactions in BOTH phases:
**die → Echo (and every on-death watcher, existing order) → THEN the Rise/Rebirth attempts the return**, which
may find no room (an overflow; the body stays dead).

- **Combat** (`killOrReborn`): the `risingReserved` slot hold is gone; `occupied` is simply the living count.
  The event ORDER was already `death{rise} → summon → reborn`; what moved is the room check — on a full board
  the Echo's `summon` now lands and the `reborn` does not appear (a `summonOverflow` fires instead). Rebirth
  moves with Rise (the 2026-09-16 "copy Rise" ruling).
- **Shop** (`settlePendingDeath`, the EMS / Cage Breaker / Graverobber path): a rising or rebirthing body is
  now marked `vacatingUid` like any other dying body, so the summon path discounts it and the Echo's summon
  takes the freed slot; `riseReturn` / `rebirthReturn` then gate on the cap. `destroyMinionInShop` already
  vacated unconditionally.
- **Choreographer**: nothing assumed Rise-before-Echo; the death → summon → reborn chain plays as before.

Old vs new, on a full board with Warden Rodrick (Rise + Echo: summon a Spear Warden):
`death → summon overflows → Rodrick returns` **became** `death → the Warden takes the slot → the Rise finds no
room (overflow) → Rodrick stays dead`. With room, both still land: the Warden first, Rodrick to its right.

**Fixtures moved (deliberately):** the four tests that pinned the slot-hold were rewritten to the new law —
`core/src/combat/simulate.test.ts` (Mama Pup + 5 sandbags), `core/src/combat/rebirth.test.ts` (Mama Pup +
6), `sim/src/set3Undead.test.ts` (Rodrick, shop + combat), `sim/src/shopDestroy.test.ts` (Imp King via
Graverobber) — plus new "with room, Echo summon then Rise to its right" and "non-summon Echo still rises on a
full board" cases in each phase. `tools/src/balance/aggregate.test.ts`'s synthetic-lobby snapshot regenerated
because Han Gover's pool insertion reseeds its draws (not an aggregate change). `docs/GAME-RULES.md`,
`docs/combat-ordering.md` and rule `R-RISE-05` record the supersession.
