The §18-B stage: the ContentContract schema frozen at v1, extraction tooling over every active content
object, triangle screening through the existing oracles, the convention deck for the owner's Sitting 1, the
rapid-fire questionnaire mode, and the language-guide schema v0. No engine changes; no parallel schemas —

## Schema freeze — ContentContract v1 (`packages/rules/src/contracts/schema.ts`)

The slice's v0 grown into the frozen v1 by resolving all 10 items on the vertical slice's schema-friction
list (2026-08-27 devlog). The load-bearing decisions:

- **Hero powers** live in the `hero:<heroId>` id namespace (`contentType: 'hero-power'`, helpers exported).
- **Amounts** are a union: `const` (plain/gilded JSON) or `formula` with a reference vocabulary
  (`stat-of-source`, `offer-buy-stats`, `per-n-counter`, …) — Obsidian Drake and Demon Agent are the
  curated exemplars.
- **Thresholds live on the trigger** (`TriggerContract.threshold`) — one home for Avenge (N), quest
  objectives, and every-N cadences.
- **Multipliers are first-class** (`MultiplierContract`, mirroring `triggerMultiplier` + `resolutionOnly`).
- **Copy policy sits on BOTH ends**: `copyPolicy` (copier mode) and `copySubject` ("what of mine rides a
  copy") — Kennelmaster carries the subject-side claim the R-AVWIN-03/04 rulings attach to.
- **Cardinality vocabulary** `'all' | 'exactly' | 'up-to'` — the `-1` sentinel is gone (validator-enforced).
- **Phases are derived**: `phaseBasis: 'derived:phaseRegistry'` marks claims the extractor regenerates from
  TRIGGER_PHASES/PHASE_EXCUSED; they cannot drift.
- **Gilded deltas** are a union: `multiply` (the ×2 default) / `reshape` (Bellringer's left-neighbour →
  both-adjacent shape change, statable as EffectContracts) / `none` / `other` (audited escape hatch).
- **Verbatim text is NEVER stored**: `textContract.source: 'index'` resolves the displayed-text leg from
  CARD_INDEX/RUNE_INDEX/QUEST_DEFS/HEROES at check time; hand-authoring is reserved for claims ABOUT text.
- **`corroborated` is DERIVED, never stored** (the `effectiveStatus` pattern): stored `reviewStatus` is
  `extracted | needs-review | approved | exception`; `deriveContractStatus` computes the machine verdict
  per run, and the validator rejects a stored 'corroborated'.

Registry split: `contracts/curated/` (the slice's 13, migrated to v1, hand-maintained — the quarantined
slice prototypes under `sim/docbot/slice/` stay untouched until WP D supersedes them) vs
`contracts/extracted.generated.ts` (rewritten by the tool). `mergeContracts` is the pure curated-wins law;
the extractor additionally never emits a curated id. New `@game/rules/contracts` entrypoint (plus deep
`/schema` and `/curated` subpaths so sim never drags the full generated registry toward the web bundle —
the D-2 trap).

## Extraction + screening (`npm run contracts:extract`)

`packages/sim/src/docbot/contractExtract.ts` (pure, deterministic, byte-identical across runs — pinned) +
`packages/tools/src/contracts-extract.ts` (the writer + report). The real numbers:

- **901 active objects, 901 contracts** (WP B exit gate: every active object in inventory, every one with
  at least an extracted contract): 888 generated drafts + 13 curated. By type: 445 cards (245 minions,
  91 spells, 81 tokens, 15 gifts, 1 henchman, + 12 curated) · 281 runes · 117 quests · 59 hero powers.
  Archive skipped by design. (The plan's 940 estimate counted the pre-archive card total.)
- Extraction confidence, visibly unreviewed (§4.2): high 377 · medium 177 · low 334 (hero powers are all
  'low' — their behaviour lives in reducer branches, listed as unparsed rather than guessed, §4.3).
- **Triangle screening** (`contractCorroboration.ts`) — per-aspect, over all 901:
  - phase-reachability: agree 361 · disagree 0 · uncovered 540
  - text-stat-amounts (T1 grammar vs params): agree 54 · disagree 0 · uncovered 847
  - text-summons (T2 grammar vs params): agree 16 · disagree 0 · uncovered 885
  - runtime-play (playScan differential): agree 139 · disagree 0 · uncovered 762
  - Derived status: **corroborated 364 (40%)** · extracted 536 · approved 1. Uncovered aspects stay
    visible — partial corroboration never reads as full agreement.
- **Disagreement queue: 0**, pinned in the lane (grow-loudly). Honest note: the first run reported 2
  (Chef Raag, Patch Job) — both were first-buff ATTRIBUTION errors of the naive compare, not content bugs
  (Raag's +1/+1 is a live Imp-Aura value; Patch Job's params carry base+step pairs), so the text aspect now
  refuses multi-effect/composite attribution as 'uncovered' instead of fabricating disagreements. The
  known REAL disagreements remain the slice's findings (R-AVWIN-10 engine violation, hero:xerox text
  defect) — Sitting-2 material, already pinned/queued there.
- Sabotage evidence (§4.5, in `contractExtract.test.ts`): a doctored runtime observation flips the aspect
  to 'disagree' and the derived status to 'needs-review'; a doctored printed parse likewise; a rogue
  generated row can never shadow a curated contract.
- The lane also holds the inventory gate FOREVER: new content without `contracts:extract` fails
  `npm test` with the command to run.

## Convention deck — Sitting 1 (`pendingConventions.generated.ts`, 63 questions)

Clustered per owner-review-pipeline.md §3 (one card per family, the R-RUNEDUP precedent): 19 presentation
timing families (the factory-keyed families in the policy registry) + 16 keyword contracts + 12 hero-power
activation families + 8 quest reward shapes + 8 global conventions (gild ×2 default, gilded multiplier
contribution, threshold scoping, token/gift/henchman reachability, henchman pricing decay, gift casts,
dual-tribe membership, combat-gain persistence). Every card is self-contained (verbatim exemplar text, one
concrete example, explicit ✓/✎/✕ semantics, member list) — format-asserted in rules.test.ts — and carries
inline `oracle: contractExtraction` enforcement so a future approval never grows the
approved-but-unenforced queue (still ratcheted at [R-PLAY-01, R-AURA-01]). The deck flows through the SAME
board + decisions.json, with the same seed hygiene (decisions survive regeneration; rejects tombstone).
**Estimated Sitting 1: 63 cards ≈ 5–8 minutes.** The sitting itself is NOT scheduled here.

## Fly-through questionnaire (`RulebookTriage.tsx`)

The board's keyboard support went from ZERO to the owner-review-pipeline.md §4 spec: a fly-through mode
(one question per screen, big key-twinned buttons, `Y`/`Enter` approve · `N` reject · `E`/`R` revise ·
`S`/`→` skip-to-tail · `U`/`Backspace` undo · `Esc` leave), a progress bar (`n of N · skipped ·
est. remaining`), sittings scoped by the existing queue chips (each chip shows its time estimate), and
every keypress the same durable `POST /__rulebook/decide` write. The list view stays. 8 component tests
(jsdom, `renderedText.mount` harness) pin the keyboard flow and the click→POST identity.
